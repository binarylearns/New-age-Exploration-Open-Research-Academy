AI Lab

Artificial Intelligence isn't magic — it's math, data, and lots of practice. Let's explore it safely and hands-on!

🧠

What is AI?

AI is when computers learn patterns from examples instead of following only fixed rules. It can recognize faces, translate text, and even write stories!

  • Machine Learning: learn from data
  • Neural Networks: brain-inspired math
  • Generative AI: creates new text, images, and code
🎮

Train-a-Model Mini Game

Teach a simple AI to sort fruits! Drag examples into categories and watch the "model" improve.

  • Round 1: give 3 examples per fruit
  • Round 2: test the AI — does it guess right?
  • Round 3: add tricky examples (a tomato?)
💬

Chat with AI (Safely)

Practice writing good prompts and spot when an AI might be wrong. Never share private info with AI chatbots.

  • Try: "Explain photosynthesis to a 10-year-old"
  • Ask the same question two ways — compare
  • Fact-check one AI answer with a book or teacher
🌍

AI Around You

You use AI every day — often without noticing. Let's find it!

  • YouTube / TikTok recommendations
  • Nepali speech-to-text & translation
  • Camera portrait mode & face unlock
  • Spam filters in email
⚖️

AI Ethics & Safety

AI can be wrong, biased, or misused. Good digital citizens know how to use it responsibly.

  • Don't share personal data with AI tools
  • AI can hallucinate — always verify
  • Give credit when AI helps with homework
  • Report deepfakes and AI scams
